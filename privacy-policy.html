Cook What?

Privacy policy — effective 15 September 2026

Cook What? is a household meal-planning app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and stored, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and name, used to sign you in and identify you within your household.
  • Household meal preferences and dietary restrictions — the cuisines, ingredients and dietary needs you set, used to generate meal plans for your household.
  • Meal ratings — the ratings household members give to meals, used to improve future meal plans.
  • Points and rewards records — points earned, redemptions and reward history for helpers in your household.

We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features: signing you in, generating and storing your household's meal plans, and tracking points and rewards. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, which processes it on our behalf and protects it in transit and at rest. Access is restricted so that household data is visible only to members of that household.

No ads, no data sales

Cook What? shows no advertising, contains no third-party ad or tracking SDKs, and does not sell your personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept only while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app, or by email if you can no longer access it — see the account & data deletion page for exact steps and details of what is deleted.

Children

Cook What? accounts are for adults managing a household. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; meal preferences may note how many children live in a household, but no child is identified.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
