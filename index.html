Cook What?

Account & data deletion

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Cook What? and log in.
  2. Go to Account (the gear icon on Home for employers, the Account tab for helpers).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you own the household, this also deletes the household's meal plans, recipes, preferences, grocery lists and points for every member. If you are a helper, any unredeemed points are forfeited (the app shows you the balance and asks you to confirm first).

Can't access the app?

Email tommy.xltan@gmail.com from the email address of your Cook What? account with the subject "Account deletion request". We verify the request and delete the account and its data within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your login credentials, profile (name and email), and — for household owners — all household data: preferences, meal plans and their recipes, grocery lists, suggestions, bookmarks, ratings, points history and reward records. Anonymous, aggregate recipe-library content is not tied to your account and is not affected.

What is retained

Where a helper account with an unpaid points balance or a pending voucher is deleted, we keep a minimal permanent record of the forfeited balance (name, email, amount, date) so any outstanding reward obligation can still be honoured or audited. Nothing else is retained after deletion.
